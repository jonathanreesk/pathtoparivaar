Nashik, Maharashtra, India

Present in person, not from a distance.

Children are best served by people who know them. Working from Nashik lets us spend time inside Child Care Institutions, sit with caseworkers and caregivers, and follow a child's journey from the first conversation to life in a family.

Alongside local institutions

We support Child Care Institutions (CCIs) and Specialised Adoption Agencies in and around Nashik with training, case documentation, and transition planning.

Within the child protection system

We work with the authorities responsible for children's care and legal status, helping case records, identity documents, and permanency decisions move forward.

With families in our region

We prepare and support adoptive, foster, and kinship families across the Nashik region, before placement and long after a child comes home.

For the long walk

Being local means we can follow a child's case over months and years, not a single visit, and stay close to families after the child comes home.

Our Work

Five initiatives, one goal: a place to belong.

Each initiative removes one barrier between a child and a family, working alongside the people and systems already caring for children.

01

Know Their Story

Preserving each child's social and family history, identity records, siblings, cultural background, and the memories they share, so every child can understand who they are and where they came from.

02

Prepare for Family

Helping children, CCI staff, and caregivers understand foster care, kinship care, adoption, reunification, healthy attachment, and the move from institutional to family life.

03

Trauma and Transition

Trauma-informed training for the adults around a child, because difficult behavior may be communication, and children need relationships, consistency, patience, and safety.

04

Legal Identity and Permanency

Supporting identity documentation, family tracing, case records, and legal-status resolution, so no child stays in care because paperwork was never finished.

05

Prepare the Family

Preparing adoptive, foster, and kinship families for the child, not just the child for the family, with education before placement and support long after.

The Path

The path looks different for every child. The goal stays the same.

  1. 1Know the child
  2. 2Understand their story
  3. 3Establish their identity
  4. 4Understand and resolve their circumstances
  5. 5Prepare the child and the adults around them
  6. 6Identify the right family-based pathway
  7. 7Support the transition
  8. 8Strengthen the family

The child's story determines the path. Depending on what is safe, appropriate, and legally possible, that may lead to:

  • Return to birth family
  • Kinship care
  • Foster care
  • Adoption
  • Another legally appropriate permanent arrangement

Our Story

Built from years of opening our own home.

Path to Parivaar was founded by Jonathan and Emily Kurapati, a cross-cultural family based in Nashik, Maharashtra. Caring for children who need a family has been at the center of our life together from the very start.

Emily spent fourteen years teaching in the United States and Kenya. In Kenya she fostered babies for two years, and back in the US she provided respite care for struggling single mothers before becoming a licensed foster parent in Hennepin County, Minnesota. She grew up with siblings who were adopted as older children, and knows firsthand what it takes to help a child feel safe and settled.

Jonathan is an Indian citizen who works in technology, supporting nonprofit organizations around the world. For ten years he has also worked alongside vulnerable adults recovering from trauma and from lives marked by crime, most of whom came from unstable homes. That work showed him how much a stable home early in life can change a person's story.

Together we fostered children from difficult circumstances in Minnesota, cared for vulnerable women and children in crisis, and walked alongside many families built through adoption. Our own family has grown through foster care and adoption. Path to Parivaar is how we hope to help many more children in India find a family of their own.

Our Approach

What guides every decision we make about a child.

Child-centered

We begin with the individual child, not a predetermined outcome.

Family-centered

Children thrive through safe, stable, meaningful family relationships.

Story-centered

A child's history should not disappear because it is complicated.

Permanency-focused

Temporary care should never become permanent by default.

Trauma-informed

We understand the impact of separation, loss, and institutional life.

Collaborative

We strengthen the people and systems already working for children.

Legally grounded

Every outcome follows the law, child protection, and the child's best interests.

Locally rooted

We live in Nashik and build lasting relationships with the people who care for its children.

Questions

Common questions.

Where is the Path to Parivaar Foundation based?

We are based in Nashik, Maharashtra, India. Our work begins with children, institutions, and families in and around Nashik.

Is adoption the goal for every child?

No. We do not assume adoption is right for every child. Depending on what is safe, appropriate, and legally possible, the path may lead to return to birth family, kinship care, foster care, adoption, or another legally appropriate permanent arrangement.

Who do you work with?

We work alongside Child Care Institutions (CCIs), Specialised Adoption Agencies, foster-care organizations, government partners, and adoptive, foster, and kinship families.

How can I get involved?

Institutions can partner with us to support their staff and children, families can find preparation and support, and professionals, volunteers, and funders can help more children move from institutions to families. Write to hello@pathtoparivaar.org, or call or WhatsApp +91 80877 60630.
